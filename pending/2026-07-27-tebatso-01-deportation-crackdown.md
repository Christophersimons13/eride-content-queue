## The news angle

Between the Cabinet-approved crackdown on 3 June 2026 and Minister Kubayi's 12 July 2026 update, **53,449 foreign nationals** were processed for deportation or repatriation in South Africa. Musina alone handled over 20,000 people. This is the largest immigration enforcement wave in modern SA history — and it's **still going**.

**Primary source:** [VisaVerge — SA Deportation Crackdown 2026, 12 July 2026](https://www.visaverge.com/immigration/south-africa-deports-53449-migrants-in-major-crackdown-under-five-point-plan/)
**Corroborating source:** [Washington Times / AP — 13 July 2026](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/jul/13/53000-immigrants-deported-repatriated-south-africa-crackdown/)
**Government source:** [gov.za IMC Update, 12 July 2026](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/inter-ministerial-committee-update-comprehensive-approach-migration-12-jul)

**Why this matters for EMA:** Workplace inspections are now official government policy. Employers must verify visa status. Even *legally* documented visa holders whose paperwork has any anomaly are at risk. This is EMA's moment.
