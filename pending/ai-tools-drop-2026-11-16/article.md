## This week in one paragraph

This was an office week. OpenAI opened a way for businesses to claim their own profile inside ChatGPT, which matters because more customers now ask an AI before they search. Google made Gemini's email summaries and drafting available to every Workspace user aged 18 and over, and launched reusable "skills" so a team can teach Gemini its own templates and rules once, including on the cheapest Business Starter plan. Anthropic turned Claude into something closer to an office suite, with spreadsheets, whiteboards, dashboards and a version that joins your Google Meet calls. Fireflies rebuilt its meeting notetaker into an assistant called Fred and made voice typing and screen recording free. Adobe added ready-made Firefly workflows, including one that builds a product ad. Several big launches are not available here: ChatGPT's Wallet, Meta's Muse phone calls and ChatGPT's Business Agents are all US-only for now.

## 1. Businesses in ChatGPT - claim your business profile inside ChatGPT

**What it is.** On 10 November OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. "Through Business Hub, businesses can claim their business, manage their information, and see insights on how people engage with them on ChatGPT" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)).

**What it is good at.** Once verified, a business can "update key details and add self-service product feeds with pricing and availability", and see aggregated figures such as how often it is mentioned and how many people click through to its website ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)). Shopify merchants can connect their store to speed up verification. You can verify by phone, business email, website domain or Shopify, and you do not need an Ads account or an Enterprise agreement to claim a business ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)).

**Honest limitations.** OpenAI's help page says availability "depends on the business's location and type", and "Local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing" ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)). So a Durban salon with a shopfront probably cannot claim yet; an online store or a service business with a website has a better chance. After the claim, you must pass identity checks with Persona, which may ask for a government ID, business registration information and proof that you may represent the business. Review time "varies" ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)). The figures only appear once enough people have interacted with you, and businesses never see users' conversations ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)). One independent write-up warns that claiming does not guarantee more recommendations, and that conflicting details between your website, listings and profile can cause problems ([Moussouni](https://moussouni.dev/post/135722)). The Business Agent chatbot that sits on top is US-only ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)).

**South African use cases.**
- An online beauty store connecting its Shopify catalogue so ChatGPT shows current prices and stock.
- An immigration consultancy claiming its profile so ChatGPT describes its services and contact details correctly.
- A Cape Town logistics company checking which topics lead people to its website from ChatGPT.

**Industries.** Retail, beauty and wellness (online), immigration services, professional services, logistics.

**Pricing.** OpenAI's announcement and help page do not mention a fee for claiming a business ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)). Sponsored Business Agents run through ChatGPT Ads are billed as ad campaigns ([Moussouni](https://moussouni.dev/post/135722)).

**Access notes.** "Any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim their business" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)), and ChatGPT is available here, but I could not confirm that Persona's identity checks support South African business records. Sign in on the web and open Business Hub from the sidebar ([Moussouni](https://moussouni.dev/post/135722)). Make sure your registered name and address match your CIPC records before you start.

**Link.** [OpenAI - Introducing Businesses in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt)

## 2. Skills in Google Workspace - teach Gemini your templates once

**What it is.** Skills are "reusable, custom instructions that guide Gemini to apply your team's rules, templates, and reference files" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html)). They became generally available from 9 November.

**What it is good at.** Once a skill is turned on, Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Slides, Drive and Chat can spot when it is relevant and apply it, or you can call one with an @mention, and combine several in one request. You can build skills from templates, write them in Google Docs, test how they draft an email or a document, and share copies with teammates. Skills can also start a Workspace Studio automation ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html)).

**Honest limitations.** English prompts and instructions only. Users must be 18 or older. Not yet in Google Sheets or Meet. Scheduled Release domains only start receiving it on 30 November, and each rollout can take up to 15 days ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html)). A skill is only as good as the template you give it, so write it carefully.

**South African use cases.**
- An immigration practice turning its client-update email into a skill so every adviser sends the same checklist.
- A marketing agency saving each client's brand rules as a skill for drafting Slides proposals.
- A salon group making one skill for appointment reminder emails in a consistent tone.

**Industries.** Immigration services, professional services, agency and creative, beauty and wellness.

**Pricing.** No separate price. Eligible editions include Business Starter, Standard and Plus, and Enterprise Starter, Standard and Plus ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html)). Business Starter is included, unlike most Gemini features covered here recently.

**Access notes.** On by default; admins can switch it off by domain, unit or group ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html)). Do not put client ID numbers or passport details into a skill's reference files.

**Link.** [Google Workspace Updates - Skills in Google Workspace](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/skills-in-workspace.html)

## 3. Gemini in Gmail for every Workspace user - summaries and drafting

**What it is.** From 10 November, Google is expanding two Gemini features in Gmail to more users: AI Overview summaries at the top of long email threads, and Help me write for drafting or refining emails ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/expanding-gemini-in-gmail-to-more-Google-Workspace-users.html)).

**What it is good at.** Catching up on a long thread with a supplier or a client in seconds, and getting a first draft of a reply. Google says it is "Available to all Google Workspace customers, Workspace Individual subscribers, and users with personal Google accounts who are 18 years or older and have smart features in Gmail enabled" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/expanding-gemini-in-gmail-to-more-Google-Workspace-users.html)).

**Honest limitations.** The Help me write version here is "non-personalized", so it does not learn your style from past emails. Google does not list supported languages. The rollout can take up to 15 days ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/expanding-gemini-in-gmail-to-more-Google-Workspace-users.html)). A summary can miss a detail buried in an attachment, so read the original before you commit to anything.

**South African use cases.**
- A freight forwarder summarising a 40-message thread about a delayed container before calling the client.
- A retailer drafting a polite reply to a supplier price increase.
- A bookkeeper catching up on client threads after leave.

**Industries.** Logistics, retail, professional services, all small businesses that run on email.

**Pricing.** No extra charge on Workspace or a personal Google account ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/expanding-gemini-in-gmail-to-more-Google-Workspace-users.html)).

**Access notes.** On by default; admins and users can switch it off. You need Gmail's smart features turned on ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/expanding-gemini-in-gmail-to-more-Google-Workspace-users.html)).

**Link.** [Google Workspace Updates - Expanding Gemini in Gmail](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/expanding-gemini-in-gmail-to-more-Google-Workspace-users.html)

## 4. Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Data - spreadsheets and boards inside Claude

**What it is.** On 10 November Anthropic said: "Today we're launching Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta, and Claude Dashboards becomes generally available as Claude Data." They "are available on all paid plans" and work on web, desktop and mobile ([Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

**What it is good at.** Sheets builds a spreadsheet with you, with tabs, formulas and drop-down menus, and exports to Excel or CSV. Whiteboards turns a plan you talk through into a board with owners and dates, which Claude can turn into a document or a deck, or send to Miro or Lucid. Data builds dashboards from a connected source such as a CRM ([Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/ai-news-this-week-for-small-business-15-november-2026/)).

**Honest limitations.** Anthropic says "For now, sheets have simple spreadsheet functionality", so for anything serious you will export to Excel. Claude Data "needs a data connector, such as a warehouse or CRM", and its certified queries and live sharing are still in beta ([Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)). There is no free-plan access.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon owner building a weekly stock and commission sheet by describing it, then exporting it to Excel for the bookkeeper.
- A founder talking through a launch plan and getting a whiteboard with owners and dates.
- A SaaS team putting CRM numbers into a simple dashboard for a board meeting.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, SaaS building, professional services, retail.

**Pricing.** Claude Pro is USD 20 a month (about R338), or USD 17 a month (about R288) when billed yearly at USD 200 (about R3,384). Team Standard seats are USD 25 a month (about R423), or USD 20 (about R338) yearly ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

**Access notes.** On Team plans all three are on by default. On Enterprise plans, Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default, and Data switches on by default on 20 November ([Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)). The charge is in dollars, so your bank may add a foreign transaction fee.

**Disclosure.** The writing pipeline behind this series uses Claude models, so weigh my view accordingly.

**Link.** [Claude - Sheets, Whiteboards and Data](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)

## 5. Claude Meetings - Claude joins your Google Meet call

**What it is.** A beta that adds Claude to video calls "as a visible participant". It listens, proposes a plan and timeline, answers questions in the call chat and handles tasks using your connected apps, with results in Claude afterwards ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-office-knowledge-worker-product-launch-ahead-of-ipo/)).

**What it is good at.** Turning a meeting into work done, not just notes. It can build a document during the conversation, and the notes, transcript and anything it produced are on the meeting page afterwards ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/ai-news-this-week-for-small-business-15-november-2026/)).

**Honest limitations.** It is a beta for Team and Enterprise plans only, and it starts with Google Meet, with other video platforms to follow ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-office-knowledge-worker-product-launch-ahead-of-ipo/)). Zoom and Teams users must wait. A visible bot in the call can put some clients off, so ask first.

**South African use cases.**
- An agency running a client kick-off on Meet and getting a draft project plan before the call ends.
- An immigration practice's weekly case meeting producing an action list with owners.
- A logistics team's daily stand-up producing a written summary for drivers who were not on the call.

**Industries.** Agency and creative, immigration services, logistics, professional services.

**Pricing.** No separate price is stated. It needs a Team plan, from USD 20 a seat a month yearly (about R338) ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

**Access notes.** Recording and transcribing a call is processing personal information under POPIA. Tell everyone on the call before Claude joins.

**Link.** [Fortune - Anthropic's office launch](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-office-knowledge-worker-product-launch-ahead-of-ipo/)

## 6. Fireflies Fred - the meeting notetaker becomes an assistant, with free voice typing

**What it is.** On 11 November Fireflies relaunched around Fred, "an AI teammate" that works across meetings and connected tools, not only meeting notes ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/11/3400748/0/en/fireflies-ai-evolves-from-ai-notetaker-to-ai-teammate-in-its-most-significant-expansion-to-date.html)).

**What it is good at.** Fred takes notes in Zoom, Google Meet and Teams, with or without a bot, and prepares a daily brief from your calendar, meetings and email. Three tools are free: Fireflies Talk, free unlimited voice typing in any app; Fireflies Clips, a screen recorder that turns recordings into guides and standard operating procedures; and Fireflies Scheduler, booking links ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/11/3400748/0/en/fireflies-ai-evolves-from-ai-notetaker-to-ai-teammate-in-its-most-significant-expansion-to-date.html)). The free plan transcribes in more than 100 languages ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing)).

**Honest limitations.** Scheduler and Clips only start rolling out "over the next couple of weeks". The email and Slack assistants, Voice Agents and FredBots are on paid plans and use credits ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/11/3400748/0/en/fireflies-ai-evolves-from-ai-notetaker-to-ai-teammate-in-its-most-significant-expansion-to-date.html)). The free plan has limited AI summaries, 400 minutes of storage per team, 20 AI credits and a 2-hour recording limit ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing)). Fireflies Fly, a clip-on device that records phone and WhatsApp calls, is only expected early next year.

**South African use cases.**
- A beauty trainer recording a screen walk-through of the booking system and getting a written staff guide.
- A founder dictating long emails and proposals with Fireflies Talk instead of typing them.
- A recruitment or immigration consultant getting a transcript and summary of a Teams call with a client.

**Industries.** Professional services, immigration services, beauty and wellness training, SaaS building.

**Pricing.** Free plan available. Pro is USD 18 a seat a month (about R305), or USD 10 (about R169) billed yearly. Business is USD 29 (about R491), or USD 19 (about R321) yearly ([Fireflies pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing)).

**Access notes.** Fireflies itself says users should make sure everyone knows when they are recording and follow local recording rules ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/11/3400748/0/en/fireflies-ai-evolves-from-ai-notetaker-to-ai-teammate-in-its-most-significant-expansion-to-date.html)). In South Africa, also treat transcripts as personal information under POPIA.

**Link.** [GlobeNewswire - Fireflies AI teammate launch](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/11/3400748/0/en/fireflies-ai-evolves-from-ai-notetaker-to-ai-teammate-in-its-most-significant-expansion-to-date.html)

**What it is good at.** It combines signals from your site, traffic, prompts, social media and mentions to find gaps in how AI systems represent your brand, and suggests what to fix in your content and sources ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/agent-powered-geo-by-dageno/launches/dageno-ai-2-0)). It pairs naturally with tool one: claim your ChatGPT profile, then check what the assistants actually say.

**Honest limitations.** Dageno's pricing page says plans cover "86+ countries and regions" but does not say whether South Africa is one of them ([Dageno](https://www.dageno.ai/pricing)). Its own blog ranks Dageno "best overall", so treat that as marketing ([Dageno](https://www.dageno.ai/blog/best-12-ai-search-monitoring-tools)). For most small businesses, asking ChatGPT and Gemini about your business yourself once a month is a free first step.

**South African use cases.**
- A Sandton law or immigration firm checking whether ChatGPT recommends competitors for "work visa help in Johannesburg".
- An e-commerce beauty brand tracking whether AI shopping answers mention it.
- A digital agency offering AI-visibility reports to clients.

**Industries.** Agency and creative, professional services, retail, immigration services.

**Pricing.** Starter USD 49 a month (about R829), with a 7-day free trial and no card needed; Growth USD 199 (about R3,367); Scale USD 410 (about R6,937) ([Dageno](https://www.dageno.ai/pricing); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/agent-powered-geo-by-dageno/launches/dageno-ai-2-0)).

## What I'd actually try this week

1. **Businesses in ChatGPT.** If you sell online or run a service business with a website, sign in on the web, open Business Hub and search for your business. Before you start, make sure your name, address and registration details match your CIPC records and your website. If you only have a physical shop, wait; local listings are US-only for now. Either way, ask ChatGPT what it says about your business today and write down the answer, so you can check again next month.
2. **Gemini in Gmail.** It costs nothing extra and is reaching every Workspace user and personal Gmail account aged 18 and over. Pick your three longest email threads this week, read the AI Overview first, then check it against the thread. If it misses something that matters, you know how far to trust it.
