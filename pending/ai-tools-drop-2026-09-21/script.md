## SEGMENT 5 - BOLT FORGE (3:12 to 4:44)

The opt out is free, any plan, in account settings.

## SEGMENT 13 - EIGHT OUT OF TEN (11:05 to 11:54)

Three vendors this week were hiding a real, detailed policy behind a non standard path. And the reverse holds. A confident no training claim in marketing copy is not a policy.
