# 8. Fact-check line for anyone reviewing before publication

Every number in this pack traces to `article.md` in the same folder, and every claim there carries an inline source link. The three highest-risk items to re-verify before publication, because they are the ones a viewer could check and challenge:

1. **Gemini 3.7 Flash price change date.** The 31 December 2026 cutoff and the doubling to 1.50 and 7.50 USD are stated on https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing. Confirm the page still says this at publication, because Google has moved these numbers before.
2. **BetterClaw free tier contents.** Three different versions are in circulation. The pack uses the vendor page figure of 500 credits per month from https://www.betterclaw.io/pricing. Do not quote the 100 tasks figure.
3. **The TechCentral 25 percent figure.** From https://techcentral.co.za/only-a-quarter-of-sa-firms-are-running-ai-for-real/284770/, a survey of roughly 100 SA tech decision-makers. Describe it as a survey of decision-makers, not as a national statistic.
