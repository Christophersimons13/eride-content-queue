## This week in one paragraph

This was a week of cheaper and more useful versions of tools many small businesses already have. OpenAI made GPT-6 the default for everyone in ChatGPT, free plan included. Anthropic put Claude inside Google Docs, Sheets and Slides and released Haiku 5.5, a very cheap small model that free users can pick in the Claude app. Google shipped a free, offline meeting note-taker for Mac, a cheaper image model, and a public website that checks whether a picture, video or audio clip was made with AI. OpenAI's Decisions API, which I flagged last week as having no price, now has one. A text-to-social-video tool topped Product Hunt. The one South African AI story worth your time, Vambo AI's African-language model, got press this week but was actually released in September, so it sits in the South African corner rather than the main list. Google's new Gemini agent is still limited to enterprise previews, so it goes under "not for us yet". Eight items made the main list.

## 1. Claude for Google Workspace - Claude inside your Docs, Sheets and Slides

**What it is.** On 6 October Anthropic [announced](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-now-works-in-google-docs-sheets-and-slides) that "Claude for Google Workspace is now in public beta on all paid Claude plans". It adds a Claude sidebar that reads the file you have open and edits it directly.

**What it is good at.** In Sheets, [Anthropic says](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-now-works-in-google-docs-sheets-and-slides) Claude "can write formulas, build pivot tables and native Sheets charts". In Slides it builds slides from your deck's own layouts and flags text that runs off the slide. By default it asks before every edit.

**Also this week.** [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/anthropic-arms-claude-with-live-data-dashboards-and-editable-code-animations) reports Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion entered beta on 8 October. Dashboards is on paid plans; Motion is for Team and Enterprise only. Claude's documents, slides and design projects left beta and now sit on every plan, free included.

**Honest limitations.** It is a beta, and it is paid plans only. "Accept all edits" mode applies changes without stopping, so leave it on "Ask before edits" for client files.

**South African use cases.**
- A bookkeeper building a monthly budget-versus-actuals sheet for SMB clients.
- An immigration consultancy tidying a checklist doc without breaking its formatting.
- An agency matching new pitch slides to an existing client deck template.

**Industries.** Professional services, immigration services, agency work.

**Pricing.** Included with paid Claude plans. I did not verify a current plan price this week.

**Access notes.** Claude is available in South Africa. You need a Google Workspace or Google account.

**Link.** [Claude for Google Workspace](https://claude.com/claude-for-google-workspace)

## 2. GPT-6 and Intelligent UI - now on the free ChatGPT plan

**What it is.** On 7 October [OpenAI said](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/) GPT-6 with Intelligent UI "starts rolling out globally" to paid tiers, expanding the next day to Free and Go. Free and Go get GPT-6 Luna; Plus and up get GPT-6 Sol.

**What it is good at.** OpenAI says answers "can include graphics, tappable buttons, forms, charts, and interactive experiences", and you can ask ChatGPT to build a small tool in the chat, such as a savings calculator.

**Honest limitations.** Free users get the smaller Luna model, not Sol. A tool built in a chat is a quick helper, not software you can hand to a client.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon owner building a quick price calculator for treatment packages.
- A logistics coordinator asking for a visual comparison of delivery routes.
- A founder explaining a pricing model to a co-founder with an interactive chart.

**Industries.** Every SMB, especially owners who use ChatGPT on the free plan.

**Pricing.** Free on the Free plan. No new cost.

**Access notes.** OpenAI describes the rollout as global.

**Link.** [OpenAI - GPT-6 and Intelligent UI for everyone](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/)

## 3. Claude Haiku 5.5 - a very cheap small model

**What it is.** Anthropic [released Haiku 5.5 on 7 October](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5), calling it "the cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model we've ever released", built for "high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks".

**What it is good at.** Summaries, classification, routing, form-filling and live chat, per [Anthropic's Haiku page](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/haiku). It also says Haiku 5.5 is the first Haiku with effort controls.

**Honest limitations.** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5) says it is best for "narrowly scoped tasks", and its new tokenizer "uses slightly more tokens per task". The 100,000-token price break matters: above it, prices jump fivefold.

**South African use cases.**
- Summarising every WhatsApp support chat at the end of the day.
- Sorting incoming emails at an accounting practice into "SARS", "payroll" and "other".
- A first-line chat assistant on a retailer's website.

**Industries.** Retail, professional services, SaaS, fintech support.

**Pricing.** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude/haiku) lists $0.10 per million input tokens (about R1.66) and $0.50 output (about R8.28) for prompts up to 100,000 tokens, and $0.50 / $2.50 (about R8.28 / R41.38) above that. Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise users can select it in the Claude app.

**Link.** [Introducing Claude Haiku 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5)

## 4. Google AI Edge Foresight - a free meeting note-taker that works offline

**What it is.** [TechCrunch reported on 8 October](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/google-releases-a-new-local-first-granola-competitor/) that Google released AI Edge Foresight, a Mac app that "can work completely offline" to capture meeting notes, show a transcript, and answer questions with a Gemma 4-powered assistant. You can add PDFs and Office files to a knowledge base.

**What it is good at.** Client meetings where the conversation should not leave your laptop. [Progressive Robot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/10/08/ai-edge-foresight-google-offline-ai-note-taking-app/) says Google released it on 6 October and describes it as "free and experimental".

**Honest limitations.** [Progressive Robot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/10/08/ai-edge-foresight-google-offline-ai-note-taking-app/) reports that Intel Macs are not supported, there is no Windows or iPhone version, and Google could change or withdraw it. Google has not said how well it transcribes languages other than English, so test it with South African accents and with Afrikaans or isiZulu before relying on it. Offline does not remove your POPIA duties: you still need consent to record.

**South African use cases.**
- An immigration consultant recording a client intake on a MacBook, keeping notes on the device.
- A wellness practitioner noting session admin without sending audio to the cloud.
- A founder taking notes in a taxi or on a flight with no signal.

**Industries.** Immigration services, professional services, wellness.

**Pricing.** Free, per [Progressive Robot](https://www.progressiverobot.com/2026/10/08/ai-edge-foresight-google-offline-ai-note-taking-app/).

**Access notes.** It is a direct download from Google (about 160 MB), not the Mac App Store. Apple Silicon only.

**Link.** [TechCrunch - Google AI Edge Foresight](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/google-releases-a-new-local-first-granola-competitor/)

## 5. OpenAI Decisions API - public beta, and now it has a price

**What it is.** Last week the Decisions API was in limited preview with no price. On 6 October OpenAI's [developer community post](https://community.openai.com/t/decisions-api-is-now-available-in-public-beta/1403877) opened it "to all developers in public beta". You ask a question with a fixed set of answers and it returns the choice, a probability or a score.

**What it is good at.** Sorting and routing at scale. [OpenAI's docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions) say it returns typed answers "about 10x faster than the Responses API".

**Honest limitations.** GPT-6 Luna is the only model, per the [docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions). Data residency is supported only in "the United States and Europe (EEA + Switzerland)", so plan your POPIA position with that in mind.

**South African use cases.**
- Sorting WhatsApp bot messages into booking, complaint or human before a bigger model replies, which matters now that API replies cost money.
- Flagging whether a scanned document is a passport, a bank statement or neither.

**Industries.** Immigration services, fintech, logistics, SaaS.

**Pricing.** [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions) lists input at $0.10 per million tokens (about R1.66), with "no cache-read, cache-write, or output-token charges".

**Link.** [OpenAI - Decisions guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions)

## 6. Nano Banana 2.1 - Google's image model at half the price

**What it is.** [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/nano-banana-21-google-ai-image-generation-cheaper) reports that Google launched Nano Banana 2.1, based on Gemini 3.6 Flash, rolling out across the Gemini app, its API, AI Studio, Flow and Stitch. [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/technology/27183) dates the launch to 6 October.

**What it is good at.** Multi-turn edits, up to 14 reference images, and "more accurate text for posters, diagrams and infographics", per [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/nano-banana-21-google-ai-image-generation-cheaper).

**Honest limitations.** The same report says small text and long paragraphs "can still be difficult", and Google notes "imperfect character consistency and occasional factual or spatial errors".

**South African use cases.**
- A beauty brand making consistent product shots across a campaign.
- A township retailer making a price poster for the shop window.
- An agency drafting social graphics before a designer finishes them.

**Industries.** Retail, beauty, agency and creative work.

**Pricing.** [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/nano-banana-21-google-ai-image-generation-cheaper) lists API prices of $0.0336 per 1K image (about R0.56), $0.0504 for 2K (about R0.83) and $0.113 for 4K (about R1.87).

**Link.** [YourStory - Nano Banana 2.1](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/nano-banana-21-google-ai-image-generation-cheaper)

## 7. Spira Maxima - script in, finished social video out

**What it is.** [Spira announced Spira Maxima 1.0 on 6 October](https://x.com/spira_lab_ai/status/2107509453889401136) as a model that "turns plain text into a finished social video: presenter, on-trend B-roll, motion, captions and music". It was number one on [Product Hunt's week of 5 October](https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/weekly/2026/41).

**What it is good at.** One-step vertical videos. The [product page](https://spira.ai/spira-maxima) says it accepts a script, a script plus your photo and optional voice sample, a script plus product photos, or an existing recording, and returns 9:16 MP4s with a caption and hashtags.

**Honest limitations.** No language list was published, so South African languages are unconfirmed. Building an avatar of yourself from a photo and voice sample is a real consent and brand-safety decision, and anything you post still needs an AI disclosure.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon turning a weekly special into a 20-second Reel.
- A property agent making listing videos from product photos.
- An agency drafting short videos for client approval.

**Industries.** Beauty, retail, property, agency and creative work.

**Pricing.** The [product page](https://spira.ai/spira-maxima) lists $0.15 per second of finished video (about R2.48), with 50 percent off in the launch month ($0.075, about R1.24). A 60-second video is about R149, or about R74 at the launch price.

**Link.** [Spira Maxima](https://spira.ai/spira-maxima)

## 8. SynthID Detector - check whether media was made with AI

**What it is.** On 7 October [Google said](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/synth-id-ai-content/) "anyone can easily check if an image, video, or audio file was made with AI from Google or our partners, including OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao, and soon, Apple". It is "available globally in English".

**What it is good at.** Spotting watermarked AI images, video and audio. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/07/googles-new-synthid-website-can-identify-ai-generated-media/) lists supported formats including JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV, MP3 and WAV.

**Honest limitations.** [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/google-rolls-out-improved-synthid-ai-content-detector-now-available-globally/) says you must log in, there is a daily quota of about 10 checks, and it cannot catch AI content from providers that use different watermarks or none. A "no watermark found" result does not prove a file is real.

**South African use cases.**
- A fintech checking a suspicious "proof of payment" image a customer sent.
- An immigration practice checking a supporting photo before submission.
- A newsroom or agency checking a viral clip before reposting it.

**Industries.** Fintech, immigration services, media and agency work.

**Pricing.** No price stated. Login is required.

**Link.** [Google - SynthID Detector](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/synth-id-ai-content/)

## South African corner

**Vambo AI MORENA.** [allAfrica reported on 7 October](https://allafrica.com/stories/202610090077.html) that South African startup Vambo AI has released MORENA, "an open-weight 1.5-billion-parameter language model" trained from scratch for 12 African languages plus English and French, including isiZulu, isiXhosa, Setswana, Afrikaans and isiNdebele. To be precise about the date: the [Hugging Face model page](https://huggingface.co/vamboai/morena-1.5b-instruct) says "Released 18 September 2026", so this is press coverage this week of a September release. The licence is Apache 2.0, and commercial use is permitted. Be realistic about quality: on that page, only 23 percent of grounded answers were fully faithful to the facts given, so this is something to fine-tune and test, not something to put in front of clients as it is.

**Google for Startups Accelerator South Africa.** On 5 October [Google named 15 South African-led startups](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/introducing-our-2026-cohort-for-the-google-for-startups-accelerator-south-africa/), selected from 1,057 applications, with up to R1 million in equity-free funding. They include Anvaya Legal AI, Buddy Learning (an AI tutor on WhatsApp) and Kasicoach (AI mentorship on WhatsApp and Telegram).
