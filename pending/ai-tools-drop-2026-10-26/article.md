## This week in one paragraph

This week's launches that matter for small businesses were about design, meetings and voice. Canva released its own image model, Craft, built for posters, social posts and menus where the words need to be readable, and it is open to every Canva user, free plan included. Google Meet's live speech translation expanded to 63 languages, and Afrikaans is one of them; isiZulu and isiXhosa are not. Perplexity's Computer agent can now run entirely on a Mac with no internet. Two voice vendors went after the AI phone agent market: ElevenLabs with new speech-to-speech models at eight US cents a minute plus usage, and Mistral with Voxtral 3, whose voice agents start at four cents a minute. For builders, Recursive released Flint, a free coding model that runs on a laptop, and Octane AI rebuilt its Shopify quiz builder around AI. Anthropic's new Claude Duet assistant is the biggest launch of the week, but it is US-only for now. Seven items made the main list. It was not a thin week, but several big launches were either outside the seven-day window or not usable from South Africa, and I would rather cover seven properly than pad to eight.

## 1. Canva Craft - an image model built for designs with words in them

**What it is.** On 22 October Canva released Craft, its "first in-house image model", available to all users in Dream Lab, according to [TechCrunch's report as republished by Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/canva-releases-its-first-image-generation-model-to-all-users-in-dream-lab). Canva's own post says it is "available to everyone" and is "built specifically for design" ([Canva on X](https://x.com/canva/status/2113348707223753165)).

**What it is good at.** Layouts with text. Canva says Craft plans "composition, scale, hierarchy, and where text needs to sit" before it draws, and it outputs up to 2K resolution in more than 30 aspect ratios ([Future Tweets](https://futuretweets.com/canva-craft-ai-image-model-launch-2026/)). Canva reports it debuted fourth on the DesignArena leaderboard with an Elo score of 1340 ([Canva on X](https://x.com/canva/status/2113348711019639190)). The output can be turned into a layered, editable Canva design through Canva Magic, as a separate step ([Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/canva-releases-its-first-image-generation-model-to-all-users-in-dream-lab)).

**Honest limitations.** The readable-text claims "have not been independently verified" ([Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/canva-releases-its-first-image-generation-model-to-all-users-in-dream-lab)). Canva has not published a separate price for Craft generations, and no supported-language list is given ([Future Tweets](https://futuretweets.com/canva-craft-ai-image-model-launch-2026/)), so check isiZulu or Afrikaans wording letter by letter. The API is announced but not fully rolled out.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon producing a month of price-list and special-offer posts in the correct aspect ratios for Instagram, WhatsApp Status and a printed A4 poster.
- A restaurant or coffee shop drafting a readable menu board.
- An agency producing first-round concept visuals for a client pitch before a designer refines them.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, retail, hospitality, agency and creative work.

**Pricing.** No separate Craft price. It uses Dream Lab, and [Canva's help centre](https://www.canva.com/help/dreamlab-access-restrictions/) says "Canva Free users have 20 lifetime Dream Lab uses", while Pro, Teams, Nonprofits and Education users get "up to 500 Dream Lab uses" a month. Note that some third-party guides describe the free allowance differently; Canva's own page is the one to trust.

**Access notes.** It is inside the Canva account you already have. No card, phone or region block was reported.

**Link.** [Canva Craft announcement on X](https://x.com/canva/status/2113348723518722529)

## 2. Google Meet speech translation - now 63 languages, including Afrikaans

**What it is.** On 22 October Google said Gemini-powered speech translation in Meet "will now support 63 languages across web and mobile devices", with automatic language detection ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/real-time-speech-translation-in-google-Meet-expands-to-63-languages-with-automatic-language-detection.html)).

**What it is good at.** Each person hears the meeting in their own language. Meet detects who is speaking what and translates "in near real-time", even when a speaker switches languages, and translation works "between any combination of the 63 supported languages in the same meeting" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/real-time-speech-translation-in-google-Meet-expands-to-63-languages-with-automatic-language-detection.html)). [Google's help page](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/16221730?hl=en) says it translates your speech "in a voice like yours".

**Honest limitations.** [Google's language list](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/16221730?hl=en) includes Afrikaans, English and Swahili, but not isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho or Setswana. Meetings are capped at 90 minutes of translation on the base plans. It does not work in live streams, recordings, dial-in calls or encrypted meetings, and is "subject to regional and age restrictions" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/real-time-speech-translation-in-google-Meet-expands-to-63-languages-with-automatic-language-detection.html)). Google does not name the regions, so test a call before relying on it with a client.

**South African use cases.**
- An immigration consultant holding a first consultation with a client who is more comfortable in French, Portuguese or Arabic.
- A logistics firm speaking with a supplier in China or a client in Mozambique.
- A professional services firm running a call with an Afrikaans-speaking client and an English-only colleague.

**Industries.** Immigration services, logistics, professional services, retail sourcing.

**Pricing.** No separate price. It needs a meeting host on an eligible plan: Workspace Business Standard or Plus, Enterprise Standard or Plus, Frontline Plus, or Google AI Pro and Ultra ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/real-time-speech-translation-in-google-Meet-expands-to-63-languages-with-automatic-language-detection.html)). Business Starter is not on the list. In a direct one-to-one call, it works if either person has an eligible plan ([Google Meet Help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/16221730?hl=en)).

**Access notes.** For Workspace, the admin setting is on by default ([Google Meet Help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/16221730?hl=en)). Both sides need to switch on Speech Translation to hear it.

**Link.** [Google Workspace Updates - Speech translation in 63 languages](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/real-time-speech-translation-in-google-Meet-expands-to-63-languages-with-automatic-language-detection.html)

## 3. Perplexity Computer, fully local on Mac - an AI agent that works offline

**Disclosure first.** This series is researched and produced using Perplexity Computer. I have included this launch because it fits this week's criteria, but weigh my view with that in mind.

**What it is.** On 20 October Perplexity added a local-first mode to its Mac app that runs "both the AI model and the agent" on the Mac, "without an internet connection" ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/20/perplexitys-computer-can-now-run-entirely-on-your-mac-even-offline/)). It launches with Qwen 3.6 and PPLX Pro as local model options.

**What it is good at.** Private admin work across your own Mac apps. With permission, it can work with local files and Notes, Messages, Mail and Numbers, for example reviewing recent emails, finding deadlines and writing an action list into Notes ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/20/perplexitys-computer-can-now-run-entirely-on-your-mac-even-offline/)). Files go to the cloud only when you approve.

**Honest limitations.** It needs an Apple silicon Mac, with "at least 36GB of unified memory for the best experience" ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/20/perplexitys-computer-can-now-run-entirely-on-your-mac-even-offline/)). Most small-business laptops do not have that. A local model is weaker than the cloud models, and web search or connected apps still need the cloud. No country or language information is given.

**South African use cases.**
- An immigration practice sorting client email and deadlines on a Mac without sending passport details to a server.
- A founder working through admin on a flight or during load-shedding with no connectivity.
- A bookkeeper pulling figures from Numbers into a summary note.

**Industries.** Immigration services, professional services, fintech back office.

**Pricing.** Included in Perplexity Pro, Max and Enterprise ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/20/perplexitys-computer-can-now-run-entirely-on-your-mac-even-offline/)). [Tech-ish](https://tech-ish.com/2026/10/21/perplexity-computer-can-now-run-entirely-on-your-mac/) reports Pro at USD 20 a month (about R331) and Max at USD 200 (about R3,314).

**Access notes.** Mac only. Windows users should look at last week's Llama Windows instead.

**Link.** [9to5Mac - Perplexity's Computer can now run entirely on your Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/20/perplexitys-computer-can-now-run-entirely-on-your-mac-even-offline/)

## 4. ElevenLabs Realtime v1 Mini and Max - one model for AI phone agents

**What it is.** On 20 October ElevenLabs launched Eleven Realtime v1, two speech-to-speech voice agent models, Mini and Max, "available starting today" ([Journal du Net](https://www.journaldunet.com/en/1556895-elevenlabs-unveils-faster-more-accurate-speech-to-speech-models/)). Speech-to-speech means one model listens and answers, instead of three models passing text between them.

**What it is good at.** Natural phone conversations. The models handle "pauses, interruptions, and turn-taking", support up to 90 languages and can use 17,000 library voices. Mini is for low latency, Max for harder tasks with tool use, and both are compatible with OpenAI's Realtime API ([Journal du Net](https://www.journaldunet.com/en/1556895-elevenlabs-unveils-faster-more-accurate-speech-to-speech-models/)).

**Honest limitations.** The 90 languages are not named, so isiZulu or Sesotho must be tested. "WebRTC is not supported at launch" ([Journal du Net](https://www.journaldunet.com/en/1556895-elevenlabs-unveils-faster-more-accurate-speech-to-speech-models/)), which matters for browser-based agents. You still need a developer to connect it to a phone line and booking system.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon booking line that answers after hours, confirms a slot and sends the booking to the system.
- A clinic or wellness practice answering routine "are you open, what does it cost" calls.
- A logistics company answering "where is my delivery" calls.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, logistics, retail, professional services.

**Pricing.** [ElevenLabs' API pricing page](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api) lists both Mini and Max at "$0.08+ LLM usage" per minute (about R1.33 a minute plus usage). For Mini, the token rate is USD 0.20 per million input tokens and USD 6.00 per million output tokens (about R3.31 and R99).

**Access notes.** Available through ElevenAgents and the Realtime API ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/de/realtime)). Billing is in USD, so expect your bank's foreign transaction fee.

**Link.** [ElevenLabs - Introducing Eleven Realtime](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/realtime)

## 5. Mistral Voxtral 3 - the cheaper voice agent option, with EU hosting

**What it is.** Mistral released Voxtral 3, "a new family of state-of-the-art speech-to-text and text-to-speech models, alongside a voice agent SDK" ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/)). [Frandroid](https://www.frandroid.com/culture-tech/intelligence-artificielle/3293341_face-a-elevenlabs-et-ses-90-langues-mistral-degaine-voxtral-3) dates the launch to Tuesday 20 October.

**What it is good at.** Price, and building a complete phone agent from one vendor. Mistral says the agents can answer and place calls over SIP, connect to existing telephony and contact-centre software, verify caller identity and book appointments ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/)). Transcription covers over 50 languages, with speaker labels.

**Honest limitations.** Speech output covers "35+ languages and 50+ dialects" ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/)), fewer than ElevenLabs. No list naming South African languages is given. Its Pro Voice cloning needs 30 minutes of audio, and [Voiceover Herald](https://www.voiceoverherald.com/elevenlabs-realtime-translation-mistral-voxtral-3-voice-clone-languages/) notes Mistral "has not published a consent and acceptable-use framework for Pro Voice". No free tier is stated.

**South African use cases.**
- An immigration practice transcribing recorded client consultations, with speaker labels, for the file.
- A call centre or debt collection firm testing an AI first-line agent at a lower cost per minute.
- A SaaS builder adding voice notes or voice booking to a product.

**Industries.** Immigration services, fintech and payments, logistics, SaaS building.

**Pricing.** Per [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/): transcription from USD 0.0035 a minute (about R3.48 an hour); text-to-speech USD 13 per million characters (about R215); voice agents USD 0.04 a minute with Mistral Small 4 (about R0.66) or USD 0.08 with a larger model (about R1.33).

**Access notes.** API access, with EU-hosted endpoints or self-hosting ([Voiceover Herald](https://www.voiceoverherald.com/elevenlabs-realtime-translation-mistral-voxtral-3-voice-clone-languages/)). EU hosting can make a POPIA cross-border data assessment easier than a US-only service, but it does not remove the need for one.

**Link.** [Mistral - Voxtral 3](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/)

## 6. Recursive Flint - a free coding model that runs on a laptop

**What it is.** [Recursive](https://recursive.com/updates/introducing-recursive-flint) released Flint, "a mixture-of-experts (MoE) model built for fast agentic coding on local hardware", with 40 billion parameters of which 2.5 billion are active at a time. [NVIDIA's blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-gtc-rtx-dgx-spark-station-sync/) covered it as "a new local coding model" on 23 October.

**What it is good at.** Speed on ordinary developer hardware. Recursive reports "more than 125 tokens per second on an M4 Pro MacBook Pro", context windows up to 512,000 tokens, and that it works with llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio and vLLM ([Recursive](https://recursive.com/updates/introducing-recursive-flint)). The weights are on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/RecursiveSI/flint) under the Apache 2.0 licence.

**Honest limitations.** Recursive itself says Flint "may need more guidance than the larger closed-source coding models you're used to", and recommends breaking complex work into smaller tasks and staying under 256,000 tokens ([Recursive](https://recursive.com/updates/introducing-recursive-flint)). The benchmark figures are the company's own.

**South African use cases.**
- A SaaS team doing routine code changes without paying per-token API fees in dollars.
- A developer working on client code that may not leave the machine under contract.
- A small agency prototyping internal tools offline.

**Industries.** SaaS building, agency work, fintech development.

**Pricing.** Free open weights. You pay only for your own hardware and electricity.

**Access notes.** Downloads are large, so use uncapped Wi-Fi. No account or card is needed.

**Link.** [Recursive - Introducing Flint](https://recursive.com/updates/introducing-recursive-flint)

## 7. Octane AI Phoenix - an AI-built product quiz for Shopify stores

**What it is.** On 21 October Octane AI announced Phoenix, an AI quiz builder for Shopify that "builds, checks and improves quizzes" ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/octane-ai-launches-phoenix-ai-171500352.html)). You describe the quiz or upload a plan, and it builds the questions, logic, email opt-in and results page.

**What it is good at.** Product recommendation quizzes without a developer. It checks "thousands of paths" for dead ends and out-of-stock products, lets shoppers answer with a photo (the release gives hair damage as an example), and reports what each answer earns in Shopify revenue ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/octane-ai-launches-phoenix-ai-171500352.html)). It syncs to Klaviyo and other marketing tools.

**Honest limitations.** Shopify only. The entry plan is limited to two quizzes and shows the Octane AI logo ([Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/octane-ai-quiz-personalization)). At R829 a month, it only pays off for a store with real traffic. Photo analysis of skin or hair is a product-matching aid, not a diagnosis, and photos are personal information under POPIA.

**South African use cases.**
- A hair or skincare brand recommending a routine from a short quiz and a photo.
- A pet food store calculating portions by breed and weight.
- A supplement or wellness store building an email list from quiz answers.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, retail.

**Pricing.** Per the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/octane-ai-quiz-personalization): Basic USD 50 or 100 a month (about R829 or R1,657), Plus USD 200 or 350 (about R3,314 or R5,800), Enterprise from USD 500 (about R8,285). There is a 14-day free trial. Charges are billed in USD.

**Access notes.** Install it from the Shopify App Store. No region block is stated.

**Link.** [Octane AI Phoenix on the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/octane-ai-quiz-personalization)

## Not for us yet

- **Claude Duet (Anthropic, 21 October).** A proactive assistant that sends a morning brief, watches connected apps and waits for your approval before sending, paying or deleting. Anthropic says it is "starting today with Pro and Max users in the US" through a waitlist ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet)), and the [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17631495-get-started-with-claude-duet) confirms it is not on Team or Enterprise yet. Its own pitch is aimed at small business owners, so this is worth watching.
- **1Password for Agents (22 October).** It lets an AI agent sign in to sites without ever seeing your password. It currently supports only Meta's Muse, needs an individual or family 1Password account, and other agents are on a waitlist ([1Password Support](https://support.1password.com/1password-for-agents/)). Team and business accounts are not supported.
- **Eleven Realtime Translation (ElevenLabs, 21 October).** Live call translation in more than 90 languages, but "in alpha" for enterprise customers only ([Voiceover Herald](https://www.voiceoverherald.com/elevenlabs-realtime-translation-mistral-voxtral-3-voice-clone-languages/)).

## What I'd actually try this week

1. **Canva Craft.** It is in a tool most South African small businesses already use, it is open to free accounts, and it targets the exact job that generic image models fail at: a poster where the price and the words are spelled correctly. Spend ten of your Dream Lab uses on next month's promotions and compare them with what you made by hand. Check every word before anything is printed.
2. **Google Meet speech translation.** If you are on Business Standard or above, or Google AI Pro, you probably already have it. Run a test call with a colleague in Afrikaans or another supported language before using it with a client. Do not use it for isiZulu or isiXhosa speakers; those languages are not on the list.
