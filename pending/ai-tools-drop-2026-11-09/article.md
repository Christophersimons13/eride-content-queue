## This week in one paragraph

This was a video week. ChatGPT's new image model can now make short animated GIFs and remember a reference photo between chats, and it reached every plan, including Free. Google cut the price of its Gemini Omni video model by 30 percent and put 15-second clips into Google Vids at no extra cost. Synthesia moved its avatar videos inside ChatGPT, and OpusClip 4.0 now edits a raw talking-head take into a finished video rather than only cutting clips. Away from video, Gemini in Google Forms can now analyse your responses and suggest next steps, Adobe's Acrobat assistant can check your documents against the live web, and Floot lets apps built in ChatGPT or Claude take payments, though only through Stripe. For South Africa, the most useful launch is free: Google's Skilling Africa learning hub, which works offline. Anthropic's new finance tool for Claude and Meta's Muse Spaces are not available here.

## 1. ChatGPT Images 2.6 - animated GIFs and saved reference photos

**What it is.** OpenAI released Images 2.6 on 4 November. It is "available globally today in ChatGPT and Codex across all subscription tiers on desktop, mobile, and web" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/)), including the Free plan ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/chatgpt-images-2-6-is-here-and-it-can-turn-your-photos-into-animated-gifs-so-i-tried-it-on-my-cat)).

**What it is good at.** Two new things. First, GIF creation: "Ask for a looping animation, or upload a sketch and ask ChatGPT to make the character wave," then adjust the playback speed and share it. Second, saved reference images: save a photo once under a name and call it up in later chats without uploading it again ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). OpenAI says it generates images 50 percent faster than before and keeps "characters, products, and brand details consistent as you edit" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/)).

**Honest limitations.** It is a small upgrade: TechRadar called it a 0.1 step over Images 2.5 with two new features, and found its first GIF "ok" and only got a lively result after asking for more movement ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/chatgpt-images-2-6-is-here-and-it-can-turn-your-photos-into-animated-gifs-so-i-tried-it-on-my-cat)). OpenAI does not publish usage limits per plan. If you save a photo of a real person, you need their permission, and that includes your staff.

**South African use cases.**
- A nail salon saving its logo and its signature colour palette, then making a looping "Book now" GIF for WhatsApp Status each week.
- A retailer animating a product shot for a sale announcement without a designer.
- A creative agency mocking up a mascot animation for a pitch before paying an animator.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, retail, agency and creative.

**Pricing.** Included on every ChatGPT plan, including Free ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/chatgpt-images-2-6-is-here-and-it-can-turn-your-photos-into-animated-gifs-so-i-tried-it-on-my-cat)). Paid plans are priced by region, so check the price in your own account. API prices for the two new models, Sunburst and Flare, are not stated on the launch page ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/)).

**Access notes.** Available globally, so it works from South Africa with a normal account.

**Link.** [OpenAI - Introducing Images 2.6](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6/)

## 2. Gemini in Google Forms - actionable insights from your responses

**What it is.** From 2 November, Gemini in Google Forms can "analyze your form responses, uncover key trends, and recommend next steps" from the Responses tab ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/get-actionable-insights-with-gemini-in-Google-Forms.html)).

**What it is good at.** Turning a pile of survey answers into something you can act on. Google says Gemini writes and runs Python code on your responses, so counts, percentages and comparisons come from the actual data. It also looks for links between answers to different questions and suggests next steps ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/get-actionable-insights-with-gemini-in-Google-Forms.html)).

**Honest limitations.** Google does not state supported languages or usage limits. Scheduled Release domains only start receiving it on 19 November, and each rollout can take up to 15 days. Users need "smart features and personalization" switched on ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/get-actionable-insights-with-gemini-in-Google-Forms.html)). Treat the "next steps" as suggestions, not decisions.

**South African use cases.**
- A spa reading 200 client feedback forms to find out which treatment people rate lowest and why.
- An immigration practice checking its intake forms to see which visa types bring the most incomplete applications.
- A training company comparing pre- and post-course survey answers.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, immigration services, professional services, retail.

**Pricing.** No separate price. It needs Business Standard or Plus, Enterprise Standard or Plus, Education Plus, or a consumer Google AI Pro or Ultra plan ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/get-actionable-insights-with-gemini-in-Google-Forms.html)). Business Starter is not listed.

**Access notes.** Form responses often contain personal information. Under POPIA (the Protection of Personal Information Act), tell respondents how their answers are used.

**Link.** [Google Workspace Updates - Actionable insights with Gemini in Google Forms](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/get-actionable-insights-with-gemini-in-Google-Forms.html)

## 3. Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash - 15-second video, 30 percent cheaper, free in Google Vids

**What it is.** Google's video model, made generally available on 5 November with "native 15-second video creation, multi-turn conversational editing capabilities, and enhanced reference following" ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)).

**What it is good at.** Cheap, short product and ad clips. You can draft at low resolution, fix a detail with a follow-up instruction instead of starting again, then upscale the version you like ([Business Insider](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/google-announces-gemini-omni-1-2-flash-1036726028)). Google says Vids users can make 15-second clips "at no additional cost in Google Vids with a Google account or Google Workspace subscription" ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)). The clips now keep characters more consistent and sound more realistic ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/weekly-recap-11-06-2026.html)).

**Honest limitations.** The developer version has no free tier ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/gemini-omni-1-2-flash-pricing)). In the Gemini app, it is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers only ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)). The outputs carry an invisible SynthID watermark ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)), so you still have to label them as AI on social platforms. In the same week, Google paused the custom-avatar feature in Vids ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/weekly-recap-11-06-2026.html)).

**South African use cases.**
- A furniture retailer making a 15-second "room reveal" clip for each new range in Vids.
- A logistics startup building a short explainer of its tracking app for a tender submission.
- A developer at an agency adding automatic product clips to a client's online store through the API.

**Industries.** Retail, agency and creative, SaaS building, logistics.

**Pricing.** In Google Vids, no extra charge with a Google account or Workspace. Through the API it costs about USD 0.07 per second of 720p video, roughly R1.15 a second ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)), or about USD 1.04 (R17) for a 15-second clip ([Gemini API changelog via completeaitraining](https://completeaitraining.com/news/google-makes-gemini-omni-12-flash-generally-available-with/)).

**Access notes.** API users of the older 1.1 model will be moved to 1.2 automatically on 3 December 2026 ([Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/omni)).

**Link.** [Google - Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)

## 4. Synthesia in ChatGPT - avatar videos made from the chat

**What it is.** On 3 November Synthesia launched a plugin for ChatGPT. You "describe the video you want, or share a doc, deck and spreadsheet," and it builds a finished video in the chat ([Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/synthesia-launches-in-chatgpt)).

**What it is good at.** Training, onboarding and explainer videos from material you already have. By default the videos use Synthesia's fastest format, with "motion graphics, avatars, voiceover, music and b-roll," and you refine them by asking ([Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/synthesia-launches-in-chatgpt)). It also works in Claude, and Synthesia says new accounts get their first video free ([Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/pt-br/updates/mcp-video-creation-from-chatgpt-and-claude)).

**Honest limitations.** The connection is in public beta, and Synthesia says "features and pricing may change" ([Synthesia Help Center](https://help.synthesia.io/en/articles/17827946-how-do-i-connect-the-synthesia-mcp-to-chatgpt)). Free videos carry a Synthesia watermark ([Synthesia pricing](https://www.synthesia.io/pricing)). Avatar videos suit internal training better than social media, where viewers spot them quickly.

**South African use cases.**
- A cleaning company turning its health-and-safety manual into a five-minute induction video for new staff.
- An immigration consultant making a short "what documents to bring" video for each visa type.
- A SaaS founder producing a feature walkthrough from release notes.

**Industries.** Professional services, immigration services, logistics, SaaS building.

**Pricing.** The free Basic plan gives 10 minutes of video a month, with a watermark. Starter is USD 29 a month (about R478), with a first month at USD 14 (R231); Pro is USD 89 a month (R1,467) ([Synthesia pricing](https://www.synthesia.io/pricing)).

**Access notes.** In a ChatGPT workspace, your admin controls whether Synthesia is available ([Synthesia Help Center](https://help.synthesia.io/en/articles/17827946-how-do-i-connect-the-synthesia-mcp-to-chatgpt)).

**Link.** [Synthesia - Synthesia launches in ChatGPT](https://www.synthesia.io/post/synthesia-launches-in-chatgpt)

## 5. OpusClip 4.0 - raw talking-head footage in, edited video out

**What it is.** Released on 2 November, OpusClip 4.0 moves the product from finding clips in finished videos to editing raw takes. "Drop in raw footage or paste a link, then switch on speech cleanup, captions, B-roll and more. What comes back is a finished video" ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/opusclip-4-0)).

**What it is good at.** Founders and coaches who film themselves and never edit. One edit can become "60 to 90 second highlight clips, a 3 to 5 minute extended cut, or a quick lo-fi update" ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/opusclip-4-0)). Recipes save an editing setup so next week's video comes out the same way ([OpusClip on X](https://x.com/OpusClip/status/2117293015308145051)).

**Honest limitations.** It only edits what you filmed; a viral hook "doesn't add new footage, change your voice, or extend your timeline" ([NovoAds](https://novoads.ai/en/blog/opus-clip-review)). The reviewers I found had not yet tested Auto Edit on their own footage ([BIGVU](https://bigvu.tv/blog/opus-clip-tested-2026-where-ai-wins-40-percent-discard/)). The free plan lists "No editing," puts a watermark on clips and keeps exports for only three days ([OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)).

**South African use cases.**
- A financial adviser recording a weekly market voice-over on a phone and posting it as a captioned Reel.
- A beauty trainer turning a 20-minute live demo into a short clip and a longer YouTube cut.
- A property agent making walk-through clips from raw phone footage.

**Industries.** Agency and creative, professional services, beauty and wellness, fintech.

**Pricing.** Free plan with 60 credits a month. Starter is USD 15 a month (about R247). Pro is USD 29 a month (R478), or USD 14.50 a month (R239) billed annually ([OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)). There is a 40 percent launch discount on the first month of monthly Pro, which may end at any time ([NovoAds](https://novoads.ai/en/blog/opus-clip-review)).

**Access notes.** It runs in the browser and takes international cards.

**Link.** [OpusClip - Introducing OpusClip 4.0](https://www.opus.pro/blog/opusclip-4-0)

## 6. Acrobat AI Assistant web search - check your documents against the live web

**What it is.** From 3 November, Acrobat's AI Assistant can add web results to answers about your documents. "Every web-sourced answer includes a clickable citation" ([Adobe](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/11/03/web-search-now-available-in-acrobat)).

**What it is good at.** Questions that need both the paperwork and current facts. Adobe's own examples include a store owner converting a supplier's euro invoice into dollars, and a business owner comparing a loan offer with current market rates. In both cases the assistant marks which figures came from the document and which from the web ([Adobe](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/11/03/web-search-now-available-in-acrobat)). Adobe says your documents are not sent to the search provider and it does not train its models on your content.

**Honest limitations.** You can only ask questions in a set list of languages, and no South African language apart from English is on it ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/generative-ai-pdf.html)). Free users get only a limited number of AI Assistant requests ([Adobe Help](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/use-acrobat-ai/get-started-with-generative-ai/acrobat-ai-overview.html)). Files over 100 MB or 600 pages, and password-protected files, are not supported ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/acrobat/generative-ai-pdf.html)).

**South African use cases.**
- A small importer checking a supplier contract's payment terms, then the current exchange rate for the invoice currency.
- A consultant checking whether a proposal's market figures are still current before it goes out.
- A founder comparing a loan offer's interest rate with current market rates.

**Industries.** Professional services, fintech and payments, retail, logistics.

**Pricing.** The US pricing page lists AI Assistant as an add-on at USD 1.99 a month (about R33), and inside Acrobat Studio at USD 24.99 a month (R412) on an annual plan ([Adobe pricing](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/pricing.html)). South African prices may differ in your account.

**Access notes.** Adobe's AI Assistant page does not list supported countries ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/generative-ai-pdf.html)). Check the AI Assistant button in your own Acrobat before paying for the add-on.

**Link.** [Adobe - Web search is now available in Acrobat](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/11/03/web-search-now-available-in-acrobat)

## 7. Floot Payments - take payments in apps you build inside ChatGPT or Claude

**What it is.** Launched on Product Hunt on 4 November ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/daily/2026/11/4)). Floot builds and hosts apps from a chat in ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor, and Floot Payments adds subscriptions with a billing page, one-time payments, tickets and deposits, coupons and refunds, plus shipping ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/floot)).

**What it is good at.** Getting a simple paid tool online without writing payment code. You test the checkout free in preview with a test card, and nothing real is charged until you connect your own Stripe account and publish ([Floot Docs](https://floot.com/docs/integrations/payments)).

**Honest limitations.** Live payments go through your own Stripe account ([Floot Docs](https://floot.com/docs/integrations/payments)), and Floot names no other payment provider. Stripe lists South Africa as an "Extended network" country ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/en-es/global)), which Wise describes as support through Paystack ([Wise](https://wise.com/us/blog/stripe-international)). One local guide says recurring billing in rand works for most cases but has edge cases around tax, some invoice features and some payout patterns ([EcomSolve](https://ecomsolve.co.za/guides/stripe-south-africa-setup-guide/)). Confirm that your Stripe account can connect before you build anything.

**South African use cases.**
- A yoga studio building a class-booking page with deposits.
- A consultant selling a paid checklist or short course.
- A SaaS founder testing whether anyone will pay for a prototype before building it properly.

**Industries.** SaaS building, beauty and wellness, professional services.

**Pricing.** You can test free. Going live needs Pro or Power; Pro is USD 25 a month (about R412), and Floot takes 1 percent of each payment on Pro. Stripe's own fees apply on top ([Floot](https://floot.com/blog/ai-app-builders-ecommerce-apps), [Floot](https://floot.com/blog/how-to-add-payments-in-floot)).

**Access notes.** Building uses your existing ChatGPT or Claude subscription rather than separate AI credits ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/floot)).

**Link.** [Floot Docs - Payments](https://floot.com/docs/integrations/payments)

## 8. Skilling Africa with Google - a free AI and business learning hub that works offline

**What it is.** A free learning hub that launched across Africa on 2 November and brings "over 15 Google training initiatives" together, including Hustle Academy for small businesses and Google Career Certificates ([Google Africa Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/unlocking-africas-ai-potential-introducing-skilling-africa-with-google/)).

**What it is good at.** Training staff on a data budget. You download short lessons on Wi-Fi and complete them offline, and it is built to run on entry-level smartphones. A built-in AI study companion called SCOUT answers questions and plans a study schedule ([Google Africa Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/unlocking-africas-ai-potential-introducing-skilling-africa-with-google/)). Translation into isiZulu and isiXhosa is being added; English, Swahili and Arabic are available now ([Hypertext](https://htxt.co.za/2026/11/google-launches-free-digital-and-ai-learning-hub-for-africa/)).

**Honest limitations.** You still need Wi-Fi to download lessons, and short lessons are "a starting point, not a degree" ([TechCity](https://www.techcityng.com/skilling-africa-with-google-free-courses/)). Google does not say which modules work offline or what the "localised reward incentives" are ([Tech Africa](https://tech.africa/google-skilling-africa-hub/)). The courses teach Google's own tools.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon owner putting two junior staff through the digital marketing modules on the shop Wi-Fi.
- A township retailer working through Hustle Academy's business-growth content.
- A logistics company giving dispatch staff an introduction to AI on their own phones.

**Industries.** Every industry on our list, especially retail, beauty and wellness and logistics.

**Pricing.** Free ([Google Africa Blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/unlocking-africas-ai-potential-introducing-skilling-africa-with-google/)).

**Access notes.** Sign in with a Google account at skillingafrica.withgoogle.com; there is no app to install ([TechBuild Africa](https://techbuild.africa/google-ai-digital-skills-nigeria-skilling-africa/)).

**Link.** [Google Africa Blog - Introducing Skilling Africa with Google](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/unlocking-africas-ai-potential-introducing-skilling-africa-with-google/)

## What I'd actually try this week

1. **ChatGPT Images 2.6.** It is on the free plan and available here. Save your logo or product photo as a reference once, then make one looping GIF for your WhatsApp Status or Instagram. Label it as AI-made. If it takes longer than making the same post in Canva, go back to Canva.
2. **Skilling Africa with Google.** It is free, works offline and runs on a cheap phone. Pick one staff member and one module, such as digital marketing or the Hustle Academy business content, and ask them to finish it by Friday. The cost is a few hours of Wi-Fi.
