## This week in one paragraph

The useful launches this week were mostly upgrades to software small businesses already pay for. The Gemini app can now create and edit documents, spreadsheets, slide decks and PDFs inside the chat, and that works on free personal Google accounts too. Gemini in Google Slides can now restyle or rewrite a whole deck from one instruction. Canva added Brush, a model that edits existing designs rather than creating new ones, for Pro subscribers. A Swedish browser called Strawberry left beta with a free plan. SalesCloser connected its AI sales agents to WhatsApp, and Empacer, a voice-first assistant for one-to-ten-person businesses, topped Product Hunt. Microsoft added more than 25 finance skills to Copilot, and Shopify shipped 150-plus updates, including a rebuilt Sidekick. The two most talked-about launches, OpenAI's texting Dots and Google's Gemini 4 Carbon, are not usable from South Africa yet. The most important South African item is not a tool but a study: local businesses are being seen more on Google and clicked less.

## 1. Gemini app file creation - documents, sheets and decks made inside the chat

**What it is.** Since 29 October the Gemini app lets you "generate, preview, and edit files right within your Gemini app chat" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/create-preview-and-edit-files-directly-in-the-Gemini-app.html)).

**What it is good at.** First drafts of real business documents. You can create a slide outline and preview the layout, then open it in Google Slides or download it as PPTX or PDF. You can also build trackers and tables that open in Google Sheets, and draft PDFs, Google Docs, Markdown and text files ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/create-preview-and-edit-files-directly-in-the-Gemini-app.html)). You can highlight a passage and ask Gemini to change it.

**Honest limitations.** Only PDF editing works on mobile; documents, spreadsheets and slide decks are "supported on the web only" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/create-preview-and-edit-files-directly-in-the-Gemini-app.html)). Google gives no usage limits for free accounts. Workspace admins cannot switch it off ("There is no admin control for this feature"), which matters if your staff handle client data.

**South African use cases.**
- A salon owner turning a voice note of prices into a clean price list PDF for WhatsApp.
- An immigration consultant producing a document checklist tracker for each visa type in Sheets.
- A logistics coordinator drafting a weekly delivery report deck from pasted figures.

**Industries.** Every industry on our list, especially professional services, beauty and wellness, logistics.

**Pricing.** No separate price is stated. It is available to "all Google Workspace customers, Workspace Individual subscribers, and users with personal Google accounts" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/create-preview-and-edit-files-directly-in-the-Gemini-app.html)).

**Access notes.** It is available "in all languages where the Gemini app is available". It is live now on both Rapid and Scheduled Release.

**Link.** [Google Workspace Updates - Create, preview and edit files in the Gemini app](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/create-preview-and-edit-files-directly-in-the-Gemini-app.html)

## 2. Gemini in Google Slides - edit a whole presentation at once

**What it is.** From 29 October Gemini in Slides can "edit multiple slides or an entire presentation at once" ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/edit-entire-presentations-with-gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)).

**What it is good at.** The tedious part of decks. One instruction can update content across the deck, change fonts, backgrounds and colours, match another presentation's style, or combine and add slides. The edits are made on native, editable slides, and you can undo them or restore a previous version ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/edit-entire-presentations-with-gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)). In the same week, Google added cell-level web citations to Fill with Gemini in Sheets ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/)).

**Honest limitations.** It is "supported in English only" at launch. Higher limits are promotional only "through at least December 1, 2026", after which per-user limits apply. The rollout may take longer than 15 days to appear ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/edit-entire-presentations-with-gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)).

**South African use cases.**
- An agency rebranding a client's old 40-slide credentials deck into the new brand colours.
- A founder adapting an investor deck to match the style of a partner's template.
- A training provider updating rand prices across every slide of a course deck.

**Industries.** Agency and creative, professional services, SaaS building.

**Pricing.** No separate price is stated. It needs Business Standard or Plus, Enterprise Standard or Plus, Google AI Pro or Ultra, or Google AI Pro for Education ([Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/edit-entire-presentations-with-gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)). Business Starter is not listed.

**Access notes.** There is no admin control. Write prompts in English.

**Link.** [Google Workspace Updates - Edit entire presentations with Gemini in Google Slides](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/10/edit-entire-presentations-with-gemini-in-Google-Slides.html)

## 3. Canva Brush - an AI that edits the design you already have

**What it is.** On 29 October Canva introduced Brush, "a new vision-language model for design, built by Canva Research" ([Canva Newsroom](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/)). Last week's Craft creates new images; Brush changes existing designs.

**What it is good at.** Changes across many pages. Brush "sees your design, understands how it's put together, and makes the changes you ask for", from a line of copy to a whole deck. It can restyle images, change multiple pages and adapt a design to other formats, and the designs stay fully editable ([Canva Newsroom](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/)). [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-canva-builds-its-own-ai-model-says-it-cuts-edit-costs-46/) reports Canva's claim that it is "up to 69% faster for general design editing"; that is the company's own figure.

**Honest limitations.** This is "the first version of Brush". Brand Kit support, referencing attachments and image-heavy layouts are still being worked on ([Canva Newsroom](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/)), so it will not yet apply your brand colours and fonts automatically. No languages or regions are named.

**South African use cases.**
- A restaurant changing the specials and prices across a set of menu boards in one instruction.
- A salon adapting one promotion into an Instagram post, a WhatsApp Status image and an A4 poster.
- An agency swapping copy across a client's multi-page brochure.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, retail, hospitality, agency and creative.

**Pricing.** It is available to "Canva Pro subscribers" at launch, with "expanded availability coming soon" ([Canva Newsroom](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/)). There is no separate charge.

**Access notes.** It is inside Canva AI on Pro. Free and Teams availability has not been announced.

**Link.** [Canva Newsroom - Introducing Canva Brush](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/)

## 4. Strawberry 1.0 - a browser with AI helpers that ask before they act

**What it is.** On 26 October Stockholm-based Strawberry released version 1.0 of its AI browser, taking it out of beta ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/strawberry-ai-browser-1-0-launch)). Its assistants are called "companions".

**What it is good at.** Repetitive browser work for non-technical teams. You train a companion for a specific job and share it with colleagues. It can run on a schedule or when a new email or lead arrives, and it splits big jobs across parallel helpers. It can also capture Zoom, Teams and Google Meet calls locally and turn them into notes and follow-up tasks ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/strawberry-ai-browser-1-0-launch)).

**Honest limitations.** Companions "pause for approval before sending emails, publishing posts, or changing records" ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/strawberry-ai-browser-1-0-launch)). That is a safeguard, but it also means it is not hands-off. Credits run out: the free plan has 500 a month, and they do not roll over ([Strawberry pricing](https://strawberrybrowser.com/pricing)). If you record calls, tell the other people on the call.

**South African use cases.**
- A recruitment or immigration firm whose companion reads each new enquiry email and drafts a reply for approval.
- A sales team turning Google Meet calls into CRM notes and follow-up tasks.
- An agency running a weekly competitor price check across websites.

**Industries.** Professional services, immigration services, agency, SaaS sales.

**Pricing.** Per [Strawberry's pricing page](https://strawberrybrowser.com/pricing): Free (500 credits a month, no card needed), Intern USD 20 a month (about R332), Part-Time USD 85 (about R1,413), Full-Time USD 250 (about R4,155).

**Access notes.** It runs on macOS and Windows. Companies can keep customer data within the EU ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/strawberry-ai-browser-1-0-launch)).

**Link.** [Strawberry pricing](https://strawberrybrowser.com/pricing)

## 5. SalesCloser on WhatsApp - an AI sales agent on your business number

**What it is.** On 27 October SalesCloser launched "WhatsApp as a new native channel for its AI sales agents" ([GlobeNewswire via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/salescloser-launches-whatsapp-integration-ai-113000151.html)).

**What it is good at.** Answering leads at any hour. The agent replies "in seconds, day or night" using your own services, pricing and hours. When a person needs to step in, the agent pauses and emails you the reason with a link to the conversation, and one click hands it back ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/salescloser-launches-whatsapp-integration-ai-113000151.html)). SalesCloser says it answers "only from your content" and supports "50+ languages" ([SalesCloser](https://salescloser.ai/)).

**Honest limitations.** No price is published. Pricing is discussed on a demo call ([SalesCloser](https://salescloser.ai/)). The South African languages are not named. You need "a WhatsApp Business account with a registered number" ([SalesCloser features](https://salescloser.ai/features)), and WhatsApp's own per-message charges come on top ([SheetWA](https://sheetwa.com/news/)). SalesCloser itself flags that WhatsApp's "terms, pricing, or availability" may change.

**South African use cases.**
- A beauty clinic answering after-hours price and booking questions on WhatsApp.
- An immigration practice qualifying enquiries ("which visa, which country, when") before a consultant calls.
- A car dealer or property agency responding to weekend leads.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, immigration services, retail, professional services.

**Pricing.** Not published. Ask for a rand-denominated quote and the WhatsApp message costs in writing.

**Access notes.** WhatsApp is "activated at each customer's request". No country restrictions are stated.

**Link.** [SalesCloser](https://salescloser.ai/)

## 6. Empacer - a voice-first assistant for one-to-ten-person businesses

**What it is.** Empacer was the number one product on [Product Hunt on 29 October](https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/daily/2026/10/29), described as "the voice-first assistant for small business owners" running "a 1-10 person business from their phone" ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/empacer)).

**What it is good at.** Owners who think out loud. You "say what you need out loud, Empacer does the work, and nothing goes out without your approval". After three setup questions, it drafts your first week of work, such as Instagram posts and documents. It keeps a business profile that remembers what you have said, and answers customer messages in your voice ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/empacer)).

**Honest limitations.** Long-term pricing is "not yet defined", and the iOS app "isn't available yet" ([Complete AI Training](https://completeaitraining.com/ai-tools/empacer/)). No supported languages are listed. It is a brand-new product from a small team, so do not move core business data into it yet.

**South African use cases.**
- A mobile nail technician dictating the week's social posts while driving between clients.
- A plumber or electrician dictating a quote and having it drafted as a document.
- A home-baker answering repeat customer questions from one profile.

**Industries.** Beauty and wellness, home services, retail, sole-proprietor professional services.

**Pricing.** It has a 3-day free trial on Google Play, and Product Hunt users got 60 percent off the first subscription period ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/empacer)). No standard price is published.

**Access notes.** It works in a web browser and on Android. iOS is coming soon.

**Link.** [Empacer on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/empacer)

## 7. Microsoft Copilot for finance - 25 new finance skills in Excel and Copilot

**What it is.** On 29 October Microsoft added "more than 25 new finance skills and partner-built plugins across Microsoft Copilot" for accounting, capital markets and corporate finance ([Microsoft Copilot Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/29/microsoft-copilot-for-finance-purpose-built-for-the-way-finance-teams-work/)).

**What it is good at.** Month-end work in Excel. Skills include account reconciliation, journal entries, close reviews, forecast reviews, expense itemisation and audit evidence packages. Excel can also summarise who changed a workbook, when, and what the effect was, and reverse specific edits ([Microsoft Copilot Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/29/microsoft-copilot-for-finance-purpose-built-for-the-way-finance-teams-work/)). [CFO Dive](https://www.cfodive.com/news/microsoft-expands-copilot-reach-in-finance-workflows/873239/) frames it as reaching from month-end close to investment work.

**Honest limitations.** The accounting capabilities are "available in Frontier", Microsoft's early-access programme, while the general finance skills in Excel are "generally available". No price is stated, and third-party plugins "may require separate licensing" ([Microsoft Copilot Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/29/microsoft-copilot-for-finance-purpose-built-for-the-way-finance-teams-work/)). Nothing in it knows SARS or South African VAT rules unless you supply them.

**South African use cases.**
- A bookkeeping practice speeding up bank reconciliations for small clients.
- A fintech's finance team preparing an audit evidence pack.
- A founder checking who changed the cash-flow forecast and reversing an error.

**Industries.** Fintech and payments, professional services (accounting), SaaS finance teams.

**Pricing.** Not stated in the announcement. It sits within Microsoft 365 Copilot; check your current licence before assuming access.

**Access notes.** It honours existing permissions, and Microsoft says prompts and documents are not used to train foundation models ([Microsoft Copilot Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/29/microsoft-copilot-for-finance-purpose-built-for-the-way-finance-teams-work/)).

**Link.** [Microsoft Copilot Blog - Copilot for finance](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/blog/2026/10/29/microsoft-copilot-for-finance-purpose-built-for-the-way-finance-teams-work/)

## 8. Shopify Fall '26 Edition - a rebuilt Sidekick, with caveats for SA stores

**What it is.** On 28 October Shopify released its Fall '26 Edition, "Exponential", with "more than 150 updates" ([Kubix Media](https://kubixmedia.co.uk/blog/shopify-fall-editions-2026)).

**What it is good at.** The rebuilt Sidekick assistant. Shopify says it "stages multiple changes at once, computes, codes, and searches the internet". It can edit descriptions across every product in a collection after one approval, answer "why a payout came in lower", and suggest prices using Shopify's pricing models ([Shopify Editions](https://www.shopify.com/editions/fall2026)).

**Honest limitations.** Several headline features are US-only or limited to certain countries, and South Africa is not mentioned. Shopify Protect's fraud tools apply mainly to stores on Shopify Payments ([Kubix Media](https://kubixmedia.co.uk/blog/shopify-fall-editions-2026)), which many South African stores do not use. "Announced" does not always mean available; check your own admin.

**South African use cases.**
- A clothing store rewriting product descriptions across a whole collection in one approval.
- A skincare brand asking Sidekick to explain a payout or a chargeback.
- A gift store generating a print-on-demand design and previewing it on a product.

**Industries.** Retail, beauty and wellness e-commerce.

**Pricing.** No separate price. It is included with Shopify plans; plan limits apply per feature.

**Access notes.** Rollouts happen store by store. Check what has appeared in your admin rather than relying on the announcement.

**Link.** [Shopify Editions - Fall 2026](https://www.shopify.com/editions/fall2026)

## South African corner

- **The first South African zero-click study (30 October).** The agency Algorithm analysed 1.43 billion Google appearances across 110 South African websites run by 71 businesses ([Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/south-african-businesses-are-being-seen-more-in-google-and-clicked-less-first-local-zero-click-study-070855a)). Comparing the same six weeks a year apart, the typical business appeared 22 percent more often on mobile but got 8 percent fewer clicks. Across all devices, clicks fell from 4.8 to 3.0 per 100 appearances. Seventy percent of the terms checked showed an AI Overview, and those terms lost 22 percent of clicks per appearance against 9 percent for terms without one. Financial services fell 37 percent. The full report is free with no registration ([Algorithm](https://algorithm.agency/research/zero-click-search-south-africa)).
- **Meta AI Academy South Africa.** The pitch final was scheduled for 30 October in Johannesburg ([The Citizen](https://www.citizen.co.za/lifestyle/technology/meta-launches-ai-academy-sa-offering-free-training-startup-funding/)). I could not find the winners published as of 2 November. Enrolment for the free eight-week masterclass stays open until January 2027 ([htxt](https://htxt.co.za/2026/10/meta-and-dcdt-launch-ai-academy-south-africa/)).
- **AI Expo Africa (28 and 29 October, Sandton).** The expo took place, but as of 31 October the organiser had not announced outcomes ([Events Index](https://eventsindex.org/events/ai-expo-africa.html)). I found no small-business tool launched there that I could verify.

## What I'd actually try this week

1. **Gemini app file creation.** It works on a free Google account, and it handles the job most small businesses put off: turning notes into a clean document, tracker or deck. Do it on the web, not your phone. Try one real task: your price list, a client checklist or next week's schedule. Then check every figure before anyone else sees it.
2. **Strawberry's free plan.** It has 500 credits a month and needs no card. Train one companion for one repetitive job, such as drafting replies to new enquiry emails, and let it run for a week with approvals on. If it saves less than an hour, uninstall it.
