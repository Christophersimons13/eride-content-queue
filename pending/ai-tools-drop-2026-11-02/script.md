## SEGMENT 13 - SEEN MORE, CLICKED LESS (10:15 to 11:08)

**NARRATION**

So what do you do with that study? Here is my read. If your monthly marketing report only shows website clicks, it will look like you are losing, even when more people are seeing you. The study's own conclusion is that ordinary search optimisation can no longer be planned or measured on its own. In plain terms: people now get the answer on Google's page and never visit your site. So three practical things. First, make sure the answer Google shows is correct. Your opening hours, prices and address on your Google Business Profile, kept up to date. Second, give people a way to act without visiting your website, like a WhatsApp link or a booking button. Third, change what you measure. Track calls, WhatsApp chats and bookings, not just clicks. Clicks falling while bookings hold steady is not a problem.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**

```
SEEN MORE, CLICKED LESS: WHAT TO DO

1. Make the Google answer correct
   Hours, prices, address
2. Let people act without your site
   WhatsApp link, booking button
3. Measure bookings + chats, not only clicks
```
