## SEGMENT 13 - WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES (9:29 to 10:12)

**NARRATION**

One thread runs through this week. Three of these tools touch your clients' personal information: accounting records, recorded calls, and whatever data your new app collects. Under POPIA, you are responsible for that information, wherever the tool sends it. So here are three questions to ask before you switch anything on. One: where is the data stored? HCode says South Africa by default. Most of the others do not say. Two: who agreed to it? Get client consent before you connect their books or record their call. Three: can you delete it? Find the delete button before you need it. If a tool cannot answer those three questions, keep client data out of it.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT**

```
BEFORE YOU SWITCH IT ON
1. Where is the data stored?
2. Did the client agree?
3. Can you delete it?
No answer? Keep client data out.
```
