## The news angle

The South African Constitutional Court has struck down sections of the Refugees Act that allowed officials to deport asylum seekers for lacking a transit visa. The ruling has **immediate effect** and confirms an earlier Western Cape High Court decision from a case brought by the **Scalabrini Centre of Cape Town**.

**Primary source:** [allAfrica — SA's Highest Court Stops Deportation of Asylum Seekers, 8 July 2026](https://allafrica.com/view/group/main/main/id/00097380.html)

**Why this matters for EMA:** This is a legal-authority positioning piece. EMA isn't just a form-filler — it's a compliance partner that tracks constitutional law changes. This script positions Tebatso as *the person who reads the actual court judgments*, not just news headlines. Builds trust with high-value applicants (business, critical skills) who care about legal depth.

**Contextual nuance to preserve:** This ruling is about **asylum seekers specifically** — not visa applicants. But it's a signal about how the ConCourt is scrutinizing immigration enforcement in the middle of a deportation crackdown. That contrast is the story.
